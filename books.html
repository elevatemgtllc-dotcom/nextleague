01 / Educational Foundation

The EB-1A Extraordinary Ability Handbook

A Student Guide to Eligibility, Evidence and Procedure

The EB-1A classification is widely discussed and often misunderstood. Some professionals assume it is reserved for Nobel laureates. Others treat the ten regulatory criteria as a checklist and collect awards without asking what those achievements actually prove. This handbook replaces both mistakes with a clear, practical and legally grounded understanding of how the EB-1A extraordinary ability green card works.

Part I explains the vocabulary, the three statutory requirements, the two-step analysis and each of the ten regulatory criteria. Part II answers 33 questions professionals actually ask. Part III collects reference material, checklists, and sources with clean links to official sources. Written by an EB-1A recipient, it emphasizes accuracy, honest evidence development and verifiability.

02 / Professional Reference

Extraordinary Ability, Documented

The Definitive Guide to the EB-1A Green Card: Law, Evidence and the Professional Record

The definitive reference for EB-1A petitions. This comprehensive guide treats a petition as an argument built from documents rather than a checklist to be gamed. It explains the statute and the regulation as written, the two-step analysis and final merits determination as USCIS applies them, and the standard of proof.

Each of the ten criteria receives its own chapter with elements, evidence, common mistakes and checklists. The high-salary criterion receives four chapters with government wage data and worked case studies. Further chapters cover recommendation letters, evidence architecture, RFEs, priority dates, adjustment of status, consular processing and what changes after approval. Every fee, form edition and policy was verified through August 2026.

From Data to Enlightenment

The DISK-WE Model of Expertise-Learning State, Progressive Experience and Impact

Where are you in your field, really? Most professionals cannot answer that honestly, because the words available—senior, experienced, expert—describe a title or years rather than the thing itself. This book gives you a better instrument: a profile of your expertise in three dimensions.

Based on U.S. Patent No. 11,989,680 (issued 2024), this book explains the patented DISK-WE method, the five pillars of professional standing, and the D.O.N.E. process that turns a career into a documented record. 04 / Advanced Methods

The Reflexive Petition

A Practitioner's Guide to EB-1A, O-1A, and National Interest Waiver Petitions

An immigration petition is usually assembled as a stack of exhibits and a persuasive letter. The Reflexive Petition treats it as something else: an interconnected evidentiary system where every element connects to every other. When one record changes, you see what the change affects. When a claim lacks support, the system says so before the government does.

This method rests on Reflexive Category Theory and patent-pending methods from U.S. Patent Application No. 19/766,919. The book includes a controlled experiment comparing conventional and reflexive petitions side by side, seventeen practitioner tools, and complete coverage of EB-1A, O-1A, and NIW categories. For attorneys, paralegals, self-petitioners and legal-tech builders.
