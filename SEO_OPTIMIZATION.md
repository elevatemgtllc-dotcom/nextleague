## 7. CANONICAL LINK
```html
<link rel="canonical" href="https://www.nextleagueprogram.com/eb1a-visa-comparison">
```

**Purpose:** Tells search engines this is the authoritative version of the page. Use if the page might be accessible via multiple URLs.

**Placement:** In the `<head>` section of your HTML

**Best Practices:**
- Point to the self (same URL) to prevent duplicate content issues
- Use HTTPS and include the full domain
- Keep outbound links under 300 total (including navigation)

## AI SEARCH OPTIMIZATION (AEO) TIPS

For visibility in ChatGPT, Gemini, and other AI platforms:

1. **Use H2 and H3 headers** - Break content into logical sections
2. **Include direct answers** - Lead with clear, concise information
3. **Use bullet points** - AI systems extract structured content easily
4. **Include statistics** - "84.1% approval rate" is AI-friendly
5. **Add comparisons** - Tables and side-by-side comparisons are indexed
6. **FAQ section** - AI platforms use FAQ schema extensively
7. **Internal linking** - Link to related pages for context
8. **Original research** - Your 84.1% independently audited rate is unique - emphasize it
